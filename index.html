Quick answers

How big is the GTA VI download?

Rockstar says the preload needs up to 212 GB, depending on the console.

Does GTA VI run at 60 FPS?

No. Rockstar targets 30 FPS on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. There's no 60 FPS mode at launch.

What does PS5 Pro add?

PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) and ray-traced shadows that the other consoles don't get. It still targets 30 FPS.

Will GTA VI fit on an Xbox Series S?

Yes, on an empty 512 GB Series S, which has 364 GB usable. That leaves about 150 GB for everything else, so most players will need to delete games or add an expansion card.

Can I play GTA VI from an external USB drive?

No. USB drives can hold PS5 and Xbox Series games but can't run them. Use internal storage, a PS5 M.2 SSD or an Xbox expansion card.

Is GTA VI on PC?

Not at launch. It's PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and there's no PC date yet.
